## 🎨 **Design Elements**

### **Color Palette:**
- **Primary Purple**: `0.54, 0.17, 0.89` (Brand color)
- **Success Green**: `0.2, 0.8, 0.2` (Success messages)
- **Info Blue**: `0.4, 0.8, 1` (Processing/Info)
- **Error Red**: `0.8, 0.3, 0.3` (Error messages)
- **Dark Background**: `0.05, 0.05, 0.05` (Main background)
- **Card Background**: `0.12, 0.12, 0.12` (Card backgrounds)

### **Typography:**
- **H5**: Hero section title
- **H6**: Section headers
- **Body2**: Descriptions and content
- **Caption**: Small text and labels

### **Icons Used:**
- `shield-search`: Hero section
- `cloud-upload`: Upload area
- `image-plus`: Select image button

- `information`: Info section
